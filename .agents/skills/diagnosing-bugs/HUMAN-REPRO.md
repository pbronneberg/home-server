# Human reproduction record

When the symptom requires a human-only interface, provide exact prerequisites,
numbered actions, expected and actual observable outcomes, and what redacted
evidence to return. Record environment/version and time of observation.
Do not embed credentials, private endpoints, or a custom interactive shell loop.
Until evidence returns, mark the reproduction unavailable rather than confirmed.
Use a local regression harness if feasible, but distinguish it from the original
live symptom and retain any outstanding verification requirement.
