# Map and decision tickets

Map fields: Destination; Notes (constraints and relevant skills); Decisions so
far (named links and one-line gists); Not yet specified (in-scope fog); Out of
scope (excluded work and reasons). Use the `wayfinder:map` label when available.
The map is an index; detailed answers remain in their decision tickets.

Decision ticket fields: Question; Type (`wayfinder:research`,
`wayfinder:prototype`, `wayfinder:grilling`, or `wayfinder:task`); Parent map;
Blocked by; Evidence needed. Record the answer as a resolution comment with
sources, human decision when required, and newly exposed questions.
Use native sub-issue and blocking relationships when supported, explicit linked
fields otherwise. A frontier ticket is open, unclaimed, and has all blockers
resolved. Research may be autonomous; design reactions and grilling require a
human. Task type only unblocks a decision and confers no operational permission.
