# Decision format

Read existing `docs/decisions/` documents and follow their filename and format
conventions. Record title, accepted decision, context, rationale, genuine
alternatives, and material consequences. Link the originating decision issue.
A short paragraph may suffice. Include status or supersession when relevant.
Only capture decisions that are hard to reverse, surprising without context,
and based on a real tradeoff. Do not create `docs/adr/` alongside existing records.
